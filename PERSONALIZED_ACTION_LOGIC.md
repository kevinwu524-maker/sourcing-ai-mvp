# Personalized Action Logic

V7 is designed to avoid mechanical next-step recommendations.

## Core logic

The system uses three layers:

1. **Business route**
   - MyyBiz Store
   - MyyBiz CPC
   - MyyBiz CPL
   - MyyBiz CPS / Affiliate
   - Co-Creation
   - Myyshop / Product Seeding
   - Sourcing Support
   - Nurture / Self-service

2. **Customer signal context**
   - evidence level
   - product score
   - client business score
   - MyyBiz fit score
   - compliance risk
   - whether transcript includes GMV, budget, proof, traffic, creators, sourcing need, etc.

3. **Personalized plan**
   - what angle to take with the customer
   - what to do first
   - what not to promise yet
   - what exact follow-up questions to ask
   - a route-specific talk track

## Important rule

Do not use all product-line pitches at once. Start from the customer's stated bottleneck:

- sourcing pain → start with sourcing validation
- traffic pain + budget → CPC small test
- reseller/distributor goal → CPL
- creator/affiliate growth → CPS
- creator with audience but no supply chain → Co-Creation
- brand needing KOL content → Myyshop/Product Seeding
- unclear resources → Nurture/Self-service
